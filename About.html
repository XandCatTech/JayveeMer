What is the Meaning of "XandCat" and what is came from?

I have always been fascinated by cats, so I decided to make them a part of my brand. The name "XandCat" draws inspiration from the SandCat, a remarkable desert-dwelling species known for its incredible adaptability and resilience even in water-scarce environments. SandCats are resourceful and dominant in their habitat. To create a unique and distinct name, I replaced the 'S' with 'X' in XandCat, making it a one-of-a-kind choice that stands out from the rest.
